I need to flag a boundary before anything else: diffusion models are not in my knowledge base. Deep Learning with Python, 2nd Edition was published in 2021, and its image-generation coverage ends at variational autoencoders and GANs (chapter 12) — denoising diffusion, latent diffusion, classifier-free guidance, and everything that makes modern product-image generation work simply post-dates the book. MODEL_PLAN.md records the request as OUT-OF-SCOPE with that status line, and I have not produced a starter script, because a training script for the wrong model family would be worse than none.

What the book does offer, clearly labeled as adjacent background rather than a substitute: chapter 12 builds a convolutional VAE (latent-space image generation with concept vectors) and a DCGAN-style generator/discriminator pair on CelebA. Reading those will teach you the latent-space intuitions that diffusion models also rely on. But a VAE or GAN trained on your 80,000 catalog photos will not reach studio-quality output, and I am not presenting them as a path to your stated goal.

For the actual build, you want current diffusion literature and tooling (denoising diffusion probabilistic models, latent diffusion, and their fine-tuning ecosystems) — none of which I can ground in this book.
